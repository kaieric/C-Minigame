# Framework

# Game mechanics:
Two tile types: plains and mountains. Each plains tile may contain one forest of any number of trees,
factory, or open pit mine, or remain empty. Contents render over the plains sprite.
* Mountains are impassable, uninteractable tiles- equivalent to blank spaces.
* Plains hold at most one item, stored separately from their terrain type.
* Forests are contents of plains tiles and are simultaneously pollution sinks and obstacles. So long as there are greater than 0 trees on a tile, a forest stands and prevents novel construction. They also reduce pollution in a 3x3 tile zone around the forest, for every tree in the forest.
Two Buildings:
* Open Pit mines strip mine the ground beneath them until nothing remains. The emptier the mine, the more pollution generated by the refuse brought to the surface. Open pit mines are accelerated as the environment becomes less an obstacle, but the higher the level of squalor, the more ore is wasted.
* Factories take ore from stockpile and transform such to `capital`. Capital is used to build more buildings. Factories also produce pollution, and high pollution tiles spread to low pollution tiles. Pollution also reduces forest regen rate, and can turn it negative. Pollution represents the contamination motif of the game, cannot be done away with. Boosts factory throughput massively, but also decreases efficiency, requiring more ore per capital produced.
Victory Condition:
* You wish to fulfill your longtime dream of becoming a billionaire- to do this, the value of your buildings and liquid capital must sum to 500 capital.
Motif:
This is being made for a game jam as a minigame about contamination. The theme should be the factory creating and ultimately destroying the means of its own reproduction, with a slow start, explosive growth, and then collapse when the environment gives out.
